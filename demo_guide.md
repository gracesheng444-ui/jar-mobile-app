# Trying the Shared Memory Jar Demo

Want to see how the app works before making an account? The demo drops you straight into two real, fully working jars — pre-loaded with history — so you can try everything without signing up or asking anyone else to join.

## Starting the demo

Go to [sharedmemoryjar.com](https://sharedmemoryjar.com) and tap **"Try a live demo — no account needed"** on the sign-in screen. That's it — no email, no password. You'll land on the **Normal** tab, one of three you can switch between at the top of the screen.

## The three tabs

- **Normal** — an ordinary jar mid-streak (4 days in a row so far), with today's cycle still open and your (demo) partner already tapped — so tapping is a plain, satisfying "drop your star and watch the streak grow" moment, nothing dramatic.
- **Repair** — the trickier scenario: 6 days of history, then "today" deliberately sitting in a missed, unresolved state, so you can try the app's signature repair-vs-tap mechanic.
- **Notifications** — not a jar at all, just labeled examples of the four notifications the real app sends (a deadline reminder, a completed cycle, a missed cycle, and a partner-activity push) — since a browser can't actually receive a push notification, this is the only way to see the wording without a real phone.

## Things to try

**On the Normal tab:**
- Just tap — watch your star drop into the jar and the streak count build.
- Attach a note after tapping, to see how that's shown to your (demo) partner.

**On the Repair tab:**
- **Use repair** — restores today's streak back to where it was before the miss.
- **Tap instead of repairing** — forfeits the repair and starts a fresh streak at 0.
- **Open the Calendar tab** and tap into any past day to see the notes left on it.

**On either jar:**
- **Change the meet-up date** and watch the star count adjust.

## Resetting or exiting

- **Reset demo** puts both jars right back to their original starting state — so you can try a different path (repair vs. tap, or just replay the normal one) without needing a fresh demo.
- **Exit demo** signs you out. Nothing about the demo is tied to a real account, so there's nothing to clean up on your end.

Your demo session stays signed in on this device until you tap "Exit demo," so you can close the tab and come back to the same jars later. Each time you start a fresh demo from the sign-in screen (rather than returning to one already open), it creates its own private pair of jars — nobody else can see or affect them.
